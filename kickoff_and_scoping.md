Project Charter
Objective: Build Spaces, shared context that coordinates multiple people’s Apple devices for socially active college students

Purpose
Devices adapt only to the individual, so shared moments require manual coordination. Spaces allow devices to adapt to group settings automatically

Scope
Platforms: iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, AirPods, Apple TV, HomeKit/HomePod
Key flows: Start a space, join a space, invite people to a space, shared state/actions, reset a space, end a space
MVP: One or two space types (i.e. party and dorm)

Stakeholders
Primary users are college students with Apple devices
Partners and teams are our own team + Apple teams
