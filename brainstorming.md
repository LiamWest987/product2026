Sprint 1


Who is your user? College students who own one to several Apple devices and spend time in group settings or living spaces.
* People using iPhones together at the same time? Common traits?


What problem do they face? Shared moments take a lot of manual coordination between devices.


Problem statement: Socially active college students in the Apple ecosystem need a way to coordinate shared moments with friends because their devices only adapt to them individually and require multi-device manual input


Spaces Ideas (Persistent + Temporary)


Crowd meetup
* Host taps Apple Watch to start a Meetup Space and picks a contact or group
* Friends who accept join the Space
* Watches give a directional haptic, and AirPods add a soft audio cue toward the group
* Space expires automatically after a set time or when everyone has met up


Party
* Host creates a Party Space and invites friends
* Guests get directions through Find My
* One tap gives guests access to the group chat, shared album, and shared playlist
* Playlist follows guests from the car to the house
* When the party ends, the Space asks everyone whether to keep the album and chat, then resets


Roommates / dorm space
* Roommates form a persistent Dorm Space with everyone's consent
* One roommate sets "studying" or "sleeping," which becomes a Space-wide state
* Other roommates get a quiet Watch haptic instead of a loud text
* Shared HomeKit lights and HomePod volume adjust automatically
* State ends when the roommate leaves their Mac or wakes up


Studying / locking in / Work
* Study group starts a Study Space from a Mac or iPhone
* Everyone's Watch syncs the same focus and break timer
* AirPods use noise cancellation during focus time and switch to Transparency on breaks
* Members see each other as "locked in"
* Only members can send urgent pings


Presentations
* Presenter starts a Presentation Space and attendees' devices auto-join on arrival
* Attendees' phones go quiet
* Slides or notes sync to attendees' iPhone or iPad
* Audience questions go to the presenter's Watch, which also acts as clicker and timer
* Space ends when the presentation ends
